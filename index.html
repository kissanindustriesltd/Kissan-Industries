About Us

Welcome to Kissan Industries, a trusted name in premium-quality peanut oil production. Established with a vision to deliver natural, healthy, and chemical-free edible oils, we take pride in our commitment to quality, sustainability, and customer satisfaction.

At Kissan Industries, we specialize in extracting pure peanut oil using advanced cold-press and traditional techniques that preserve the oil’s natural flavor, aroma, and nutritional value. Our facility is equipped with state-of-the-art machinery and follows stringent quality control processes at every stage—from sourcing high-grade peanuts to final packaging.

We work closely with local farmers to source the finest peanuts, ensuring fair trade practices and supporting community development. Our peanut oil is rich in heart-healthy fats, antioxidants, and essential nutrients, making it a preferred choice for health-conscious consumers and culinary professionals alike.

With years of experience and a passion for excellence, we continue to innovate and expand, offering products that meet the highest standards of purity and taste. Whether you're a wholesaler, retailer, or direct customer, we’re committed to delivering the best.

Our Products

100% Pure Peanut Oil

Our signature product, 100% pure peanut oil, is cold-pressed to retain all the natural nutrients and flavors. Perfect for cooking, frying, and dressing, it adds a rich, nutty taste to your favorite dishes.

Available Sizes: 250ml, 500ml, 1L, 5L

Ideal For: Home cooking, frying, sautéing

Refined Peanut Oil

Our refined peanut oil offers a higher smoke point, making it ideal for deep frying, grilling, and high-heat cooking. It’s carefully filtered for a clear, neutral oil that brings out the best in your food without overpowering flavors.

Available Sizes: 1L, 5L, 10L

Ideal For: Commercial kitchens, deep frying

Organic Peanut Oil

For those seeking an all-natural, health-conscious option, our organic peanut oil is certified 100% organic and cold-pressed, ensuring that it is free from pesticides and chemicals.

Available Sizes: 250ml, 500ml, 1L

Ideal For: Health-conscious consumers, organic cooking

Peanut Oil for Cosmetics

Our premium-grade peanut oil, rich in vitamins E and B, is ideal for cosmetic and skin care products. It is a nourishing base for lotions, soaps, and other beauty products, providing deep moisture and care for your skin.

Available Sizes: 500ml, 1L

Ideal For: Skincare, cosmetics industry

Our Process

At Kissan Industries, we follow a meticulous and quality-focused process to ensure that every drop of our peanut oil is pure, natural, and rich in nutrients. Here’s how we do it:

Sourcing High-Quality Peanuts

We begin by carefully selecting the finest, pesticide-free peanuts sourced directly from trusted local farmers. Only the best-grade peanuts are approved for processing.

Quality Control

Every batch undergoes rigorous quality checks in our in-house lab to meet safety and quality standards, assuring our customers of pure and premium-grade peanut oil.

Packaging

The purified oil is hygienically packed in food-grade containers of various sizes, ensuring a longer shelf life without the need for artificial preservatives.
